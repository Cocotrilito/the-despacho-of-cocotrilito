About me

My name is Isaac, Isaac means "the one who laughs." I'm a 16 year old currently in high school. I have a double life: in the morning I'm a normal student, I laugh with my friends, and I love answering the teacher's questions. After three o'clock my life changes. I try to pursue a life I am proud of. The afternoon starts with a gym session, I return home and give everything to accomplish my dream. I study for the SAT while I build a bigger portfolio. I love making things, I love learning, and I love the feeling of almost failing, of racing against time, pushing myself to get everything done fast. I love that feeling because it means I have the chance to come back and prove it right... I get faster, I stop making the same mistakes. I learn.
